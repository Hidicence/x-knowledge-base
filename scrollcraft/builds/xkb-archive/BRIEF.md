## Eight decisions

1. Vibe: sculptural, precise, editorial, surprising. Authored direction: a contemporary archive exhibition with silver, white, ink, and vermilion.
2. Journey: encounter the archive; recognize lost context; follow one piece of evidence through collection, review, and recall; inspect its provenance; choose an agent and begin.
3. Energy: striking opening, quiet thesis, one large kinetic assembly, focused reading, confident arrival.
4. Feeling curve: curiosity from independent photographic planes; recognition from a plain sentence; discovery from sources assembling into one inspectable record; trust from an evidence trail; ownership from a usable setup selection.
5. Signature: the Evidence Thread. A red physical strand from the generated hero becomes a drawn path through real HTML source fragments. Scroll assembles these into a card, routes it through review, and carries its citation into a shared recall answer. All example content is clearly labeled.
6. Range: expressive editorial, beyond premium-minimal; restrained typography and usable controls.
7. Distinct scenes, authored decision under delegation. No continuous-world claim.
8. Assets: README graphics are reference material, not suitable photographic layers. Generate new concept imagery with GPT Image 2.5. Existing code and docs provide factual copy.

## Grammar

Custom grammar: Archive exhibition. A full-bleed dimensional title encounter, a quiet editorial thesis, one interactive evidence assembly, an unframed evidence ledger, and a setup desk. A bottom contents dock replaces the former top navigation. A scene is an encounter with a different knowledge artifact. Ban repeating chapter crossfades, numbered section counters, scroll cues, feature-card grids, and magnetic CTAs.

The predefined grammars do not fit completely: filmic one-shot bans useful jumping and hard cuts; chaptered editorial bans the required photographic hero; live surface would imply a working backend; continuous world falsely implies geography; typographic poster excludes commissioned media; gallery is a range rather than a lifecycle; split stage suggests a binary comparison; rhythmic cutlist cannot support the quiet evidence assembly. The custom grammar states its own navigation, order, ending, and bans.

## Layer contract

- Environment: bright neutral architectural archive plate, no focal sculpture; small scale/translation.
- Subject: genuine-alpha polished metal archive knot with vermilion thread, independent medium scale, floating by intent with no grounding contact.
- Foreground: separate genuine-alpha archival sheets, greater translation, never crossing essential text.
- Typography: semantic HTML title between environment and subject; bottom portion may be occluded, brand remains immediately readable.
- Mobile: title above subject; reduced travel; foreground omitted if it compromises reading. Asset failures retain complete semantic content.

Opening: XKB title dominates a bright sculptural archive. Midpoint: the subject advances while foreground sheets move outward and the title recedes. Exit: the image leaves naturally and a short thesis arrives. No blank pinned tail.
