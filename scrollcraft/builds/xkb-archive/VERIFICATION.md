# Verification / XKB archive

## Design result

The custom Archive exhibition grammar replaces the prior dark graph presentation. Bottom contents navigation, layered generated imagery, a single evidence-assembly peak, an expandable source ledger, and a functional setup desk differ from the prior build on all six fingerprint axes.

Feeling check: curiosity -> recognition -> discovery -> trust -> ownership. The initial visual pass exposed hidden hero copy caused by a worldflight-only engine attribute. The page now explicitly paints its own hero copy. Phone subject size was reduced to preserve the full introduction; the review stamp moved below card text. These corrections preserve the intended curve rather than changing the brief to match defects.

## Assets

All three PNG originals are retained under `site/public/assets/`. The sculpture and foreground images have genuine alpha channels (0..255 and 0..254 respectively); the architectural plate is RGB. Delivery WebPs preserve alpha. The three delivery images load successfully at all tested viewport sizes. The images are conceptual artwork, not a screenshot of a functioning XKB interface.

Built-in image generation produced the final assets. Requested Image 2.5 via Higgsfield was rejected by its service-plan gate; no jobs were created there. Full prompts and generation notes are in ASSETS.md.
